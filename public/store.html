Hand us your strategy and we turn it into an Expert Advisor. We book a call, you pay a 50% deposit, and the rest is due on delivery.

Strategy hand-in guidelines

  1. Clear entry rules. Exactly what must happen before a trade opens.
  2. Clear exit rules. Stop loss, take profit, trailing or time exits.
  3. Risk management. Lot sizing, risk per trade, daily limits.
  4. Pairs and timeframe. Which instruments and which chart timeframe.
  5. Indicators and settings. Name every indicator with its exact values.
  6. Examples. Screenshots of a few winning and losing setups (send by email after booking).

A vague or incomplete strategy cannot be built, so please be specific. We'll confirm everything on the call.
